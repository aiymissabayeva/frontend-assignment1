About Zendaya

Born: September 1, 1996

Zendaya is an American actress and singer. She began her career as a child model and dancer before becoming famous through Disney Channel. She is known for her roles in Euphoria, Spider-Man, and Dune. Zendaya has received several awards for her acting and has become one of the most influential young actresses in Hollywood.
